wafer.space Run 1

Private designs

Open source is welcome on wafer.space, but not required.

Many of the designs on wafer.space Run 1 are open source. Their makers published them, so we measure and show each one on the run's page for anyone to learn from.

2 of the 40 slots on this reticle hold private designs. They were made on the same wafers as the published ones, but their owners chose not to publish them. In the run's public layout these slots are scrambled to hide what is inside, and the project list shows only a short code and the slot size. So we show them here as locked slots and don't measure them.

Your design can stay private

The GF180MCU process design kit (the PDK) is open source, but your design can be open or closed. You don't have to publish its layout or its source, and you don't need to sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) to get started. On wafer.space Run 1, private and published designs shared the same reticle.

Private here just means the design files aren't published. Open-source designs can be commercial too.

Questions about keeping a design private? Email info@wafer.space.
