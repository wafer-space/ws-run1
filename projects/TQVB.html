This design started on Tiny Tapeout. You can too!

TinyQV RISC-V SoC was made on Tiny Tapeout 6 before it had a slot of its own on a wafer.space run. Tiny Tapeout puts many small designs on one chip, so a first version costs a fraction of a whole die: try the idea on a Tiny Tapeout tile, then bring it to wafer.space when it needs the room.
