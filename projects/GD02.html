An SoC based off the award-winning SERV, the world’s smallest RISC-V CPU. With all the room available in the full-slot we can fit 9 cores connected to local SRAM. Each core is loaded from SPI FLASH on boot, and connected to some peripherals. A queue peripheral enables both synchronisation and core-core communications.
