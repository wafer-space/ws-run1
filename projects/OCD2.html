ocd_sram_test

OCD2 · Memory test chip · 1 × ½ slot

Rendered from the layout at 2.5 µm per pixel. Layers: NwellDiffusionPolysiliconMetal 1Metal 2Metal 3Metal 4Metal 5Top metalPad openings

Test chip for the 3.3V SRAM macros (256 byte, 512 byte, and 1kbyte) from the library gf180mcu_ocd_ip_sram.

The test chip consists of one each of the 1kB and 512B SRAM cores and two 256B cores pinned out to individual GPIO pins.

Also there is a POR macro with a test output.
