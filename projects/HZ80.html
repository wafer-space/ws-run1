This design started on Tiny Tapeout. You can too!

Open-source Z80 was made on Tiny Tapeout 7 before it had a slot of its own on a wafer.space run. Tiny Tapeout puts many small designs on one chip, so a first version costs a fraction of a whole die: try the idea on a Tiny Tapeout tile, then bring it to wafer.space when it needs the room.

Magic DRC is advisory: its count does not stop a design. In the logs, Magic's "Error while reading cell" lines about fill cells are messages from its reader, not check failures.
