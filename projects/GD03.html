GF180MCU Run 1

Racquet r2p0 - 23 core SoC

GD03 · RISC-V and CPUs · 1 × 1 slot

Rendered from the layout at 2.5 µm per pixel. Layers: NwellDiffusionPolysiliconMetal 1Metal 2Metal 3Metal 4Metal 5Top metalPad openings

An SoC based off the award-winning SERV, the world’s smallest RISC-V CPU. With all the room available in the full-slot we can fit 23 cores connected to local SRAM. Each core is loaded from SPI FLASH on boot, and connected to a slew of peripherals: uarts, timers and more uarts. A queue peripheral enables both synchronisation and core-core communications.
