How this design grew

Avalon Semiconductors' retro-computing family has been made on 5 shuttles since Dec 2022. Every version, oldest first, with this one in bold:

  1. Dec 2022 · Tiny Tapeout 2 Avalon Semiconductors '5401' 4-bit Microprocessor — 1×1 tile, a 4-bit microprocessor Avalon Semiconductors 'TBB1143' Programmable Sound Generator — 1×1 tile, a programmable sound generator
  2. Dec 2022 · Google GFMPW-0 105A AS2650 — Caravel chip, a Signetics 2650 8-bit CPU replica
  3. Apr 2023 · Tiny Tapeout 3 Avalon Semiconductors '5401' 4-bit Microprocessor — 1×1 tile, a 4-bit microprocessor Avalon Semiconductors 'TBB1143' Programmable Sound Generator — 1×1 tile, a programmable sound generator
  4. Dec 2025 · wafer.space Run 1 AS03 WS-Multi — 1×1 slot, a multi-project die: 6502 and SID replicas, an RV32IMA core, a DRAM controller and more
  5. Jul 2026 · wafer.space Run 2 AS04 AS04 Multi-Project die using 3.3V SCL — 1×1 slot, the multi-project die again, on 3.3 V standard cells

Tiny Tapeout tile Google shuttle wafer.space die

Early versions were made on Tiny Tapeout. You can start there too!

Avalon Semiconductors' retro-computing family was made on Tiny Tapeout 2 alongside its first full-size chip. Tiny Tapeout puts many small designs on one chip, so a first version costs a fraction of a whole die: try the idea on a Tiny Tapeout tile, then bring it to wafer.space when it needs the room.
